Problem: $ARGUMENTS

1. **Options**: give 2–4 genuinely different options (including «do nothing» or «reuse what already exists» if it makes sense). Read `.mission/plan.md` to stay close to the goal, users, source of truth, constraints.
2. **Compare**: one table — criteria (fits the need, effort, service cost, risk, ability to grow, one source of truth, security, who maintains it) × options.
3. **Check**: for facts that can be checked (prices, service limits, laws), look them up instead of guessing; note the source.
4. **Critique**: call `mission-checker` (technical gaps/risks) and `mission-evaluator` (which one would a real user choose, what has not been thought of yet) in parallel, giving them the comparison table — not your recommendation.
5. **Review**: update the table based on the critique.
6. **Recommend**: one option + reasons + the conditions under which you would change your mind. The final decision is the project owner's; when the project owner decides, record it under «Decisions made» in the plan.
Present it briefly, in plain English, preferring tables.
